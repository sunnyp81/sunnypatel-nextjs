# Hero concepts v3: one readable idea per post

Rule: every image is one familiar object a non-SEO reader already understands (chart, podium, calendar, scale, shield, folder, tag, chat bubble, browser window, steps, pillars) plus one clear cause-and-effect. No random tiles, no leader lines, no empty circles, no decoration that carries no meaning. Blue is the thing the post is about. Gold is the result or the win. Everything else is grey.

Style, locked after Sunny's review of H08, H11 and H12 (approved frames and their exact prompts in `docs/design/approved-v3/`):
- Minimal: the fewest objects that carry the idea, never more than three object groups, at least half the frame empty.
- Literal: show the thing itself happening (coins on a scale, the page pinned under the AI answer), never a symbol standing in for it. When a real interface exists that people already recognise (search bar, answer card, chat bubble, browser window, calendar page, price tag, bar chart), draw that.
- Flat vector, polished, crisp edges, soft contact shadows, dark charcoal ground, upper third quiet. Isometric or straight-on, whichever reads faster. Browser window dots always grey. Slick, not diagram scribble.
- Rejected on sight: a line from a bubble to one window in a row (too abstract), a mini page inside a bubble (too fussy). If a concept needs a caption to work, it is wrong.
