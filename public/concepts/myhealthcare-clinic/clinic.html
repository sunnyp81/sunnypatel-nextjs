Questions about the Fulham clinic

How do I book an appointment in Fulham?

Book online and choose Fulham as your clinic, call 020 7971 1787, or ask at reception when you visit.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

We try to see urgent concerns on the same day whenever we can. Check online early in the day for the best choice of times.

Can I ask for a specific GP or dentist?

Yes. Ask when you book and we will do our best to arrange it.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. Check your cover with your insurer first. Usually you pay us and then claim the cost back.

What should I bring to my first GP appointment?

Any relevant medical records, a list of the medicines you take, and your insurance details if you have them.
