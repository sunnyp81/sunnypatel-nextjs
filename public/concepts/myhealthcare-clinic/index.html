Questions before you book

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking tool to choose your clinic and a time that suits you. You create a free account the first time you book. You can also call your clinic on 020 7971 1787.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Same-day GP appointments are often available in clinic or online. Check online early in the day for the best choice of times.

Are my records available at every clinic?

Yes. Your records are available at all MyHealthcare Clinic locations so any clinic can see your latest information.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. Check your cover with your insurer before your appointment. Usually you pay us and then claim the cost back from your insurer.
